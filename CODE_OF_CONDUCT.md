# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Kevin OS Model is intended to be a constructive place to share and improve open resources. Participants should be treated with respect, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or preferred AI platform.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when giving feedback.
- Discuss ideas and work, not personal worth.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to correcting mistakes.
- Respect privacy, consent, licenses, and attribution.
- Keep discussions relevant and make space for different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Include relevant context and links, and avoid reposting private information publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. This document applies to project spaces and project-related interactions.
